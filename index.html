Trusted contact & successor advisor

A trusted contact is someone your custodian may reach if they suspect diminished capacity or can't reach you (FINRA Rule 4512). A successor advisor is who steps in to manage the plan if your primary advisor — or you — becomes incapacitated. Designate both now, while you can.

Pressure-test your retirement date

Delaying full retirement by even two to three years can dramatically improve plan sustainability — more years of contributions and growth, fewer years of withdrawals, and a larger Social Security benefit. Enter your numbers to see the difference.
